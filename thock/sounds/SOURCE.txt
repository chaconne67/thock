Both loops use this recording's public HQ MP3 preview:
https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/815/815614_15899953-hq.mp3
The source's original MP3 download requires a Freesound login.

The natural sequence of different key strikes and its changing rhythm are retained.
The preview was decoded to mono 48 kHz, 16-bit PCM. No single strike was copied
and repeated; no pitch, speed, or equalization change was applied.

recording.wav: source 0.360-58.261 seconds; 12 long pauses shortened; final
53.001 seconds, -23.8 dB gain. Intended as quiet sound during microphone use.
processing.wav: source 2.000-20.300 seconds; 1 long pause shortened; final
17.830 seconds, -11.8 dB gain. Intended for transcription/correction wait.

A long pause is a gap over 0.42 seconds between active 10 ms frames
(frame RMS above -36 dBFS or peak above -34 dBFS). Each edited gap keeps
0.11 seconds after the preceding strike and before the next strike; the cut
has a 20 ms linear crossfade. Shorter intervals are preserved. Each file has
a 90 ms sine fade-in and a 220 ms cosine fade-out, including zero endpoints.

HHKB: recording uses source 0.00-44.70 s (44.70 s); processing uses
2.00-21.00 s (19.00 s). All are mono 48 kHz, 16-bit PCM.
No individual hit was copied or sequenced. Each recording's own varied typing
cadence remains. Each has a 90 ms sine fade-in, a 220 ms cosine fade-out and
zero endpoints. Loudness was matched to the existing Rainy75 phase tracks
(approximately -59.9 dBFS RMS while recording; -46.9 dBFS RMS while processing)
to avoid a sudden loudness jump when switching keyboards. No speed or pitch
changes or noise reduction were applied.

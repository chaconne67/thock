Both loops are derived from a real Rainy75 keyboard strike at 35.204-35.275 seconds in
the public HQ MP3 preview:
https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/815/815614_15899953-hq.mp3
The source's original MP3 download requires a Freesound login.

The single strike was made mono 48 kHz 16-bit PCM and trimmed before the next
separate impact. It has short fades, 55 Hz high-pass, +2.5 dB at 230 Hz,
-3 dB at 3.5 kHz and 9 kHz low-pass, with 5 dB output gain.
No pitch shift, synthesis or other keyboard recording was used.

recording.wav: 6.0-second seamless loop, 15 irregularly spaced strikes,
approximately -24 dBFS peak. The quiet level is intended for microphone use.
processing.wav: 2.4-second seamless loop, 11 faster strikes,
approximately -12 dBFS peak. It ends when pasting/processing finishes.

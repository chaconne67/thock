A long pause is a gap over 0.42 seconds between active 10 ms frames
(frame RMS above -36 dBFS or peak above -34 dBFS). Each edited gap keeps
0.11 seconds after the preceding strike and before the next strike; the cut
has a 20 ms linear crossfade. Shorter intervals are preserved. Each file has
a 90 ms sine fade-in and a 220 ms cosine fade-out, including zero endpoints.

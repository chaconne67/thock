Fonts for the live preview box, loaded privately by Thock (not installed into Windows).
All are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see the OFL-*.txt next to each font);
the OFL allows bundling them with software, sold or not, but not selling them alone.

Korean (each also has Latin letters)
- NotoSansKR-Regular.ttf (Noto Sans KR, Source Han Sans / 본고딕): google/fonts
  ofl/notosanskr/NotoSansKR[wght].ttf, static instance at wght 400 made with
  fontTools varLib.instancer (GDI+ draws only static TrueType fonts).
- Pretendard-Regular.ttf: orioncactus/pretendard release v1.3.9,
  public/static/alternative/Pretendard-Regular.ttf, unchanged.
- NanumBarunGothic.ttf (나눔바른고딕): NAVER hangeul.naver.com
  webfont/zips/nanum-barun-gothic.zip, NanumBarunGothic.ttf, unchanged. NAVER
  publishes the Nanum fonts under the SIL OFL 1.1 ("오픈 라이선스, 상업적 사용 허용").

English (Latin only; used when the dictated text has no Hangul)
- Inter-Regular.ttf: google/fonts ofl/inter/Inter[opsz,wght].ttf, instance at wght 400, opsz 14.
- Roboto-Regular.ttf: google/fonts ofl/roboto/Roboto[wdth,wght].ttf, instance at wght 400, wdth 100.
- SourceSans3-Regular.ttf: google/fonts ofl/sourcesans3/SourceSans3[wght].ttf, instance at wght 400.
